# Incident MD-414: release triage

Hannah, platform product. I can't build the code, so I worked from the
reports, the release's commit list (`git log main..interview/md-414-exercise`)
and what each commit says it changes.

## Recommendation

**Roll the release back to the last good build today.** Twenty commits went
out together with CI red. Five reports touch money, compliance or our
exchange relationship, and we cannot tell which other commits are safe.
Until the rollback is live: trading stays off on FIRM01, and Operations uses
the risk system's PnL, not `report.py`.

## What I did

button, because their commit messages say exactly what changed and the
reports match. The other commits need an engineer.
