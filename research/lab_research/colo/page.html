Option C Colo Path

The lab's firm placed in an exchange colo: the feed arrives over a cross-connect, an Arista 7130 fans it out on Layer 1 circuits to kernel-bypass hosts, and MetaMux merges their orders back toward the matching engine. Every configuration here ran on the identical market-data stream, so every tick is a fair race.
